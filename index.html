Damp & DPC

Understand the cause.
Choose the right treatment.

Not all damp is the same. Understanding where moisture comes from helps determine what works may be suitable.

Condensation

Moisture in indoor air can collect on colder surfaces. Poor ventilation can make the problem worse.

Penetrating damp

Water can enter through external defects in the building fabric. A survey helps identify the likely route of moisture.

Rising damp

Moisture near the base of a wall may indicate rising damp, but an assessment is needed before selecting treatment.

Treatment & remedial options

Depending on survey findings, A2Z describes the following possible works.

Ventilation

Vents and airflow improvements where appropriate.

DPC works

Internal damp-proof course injection or external DPC works.

Plaster & membranes

Removal of affected plaster and suitable membrane installation.

Remedial works

Make good affected areas as part of the agreed treatment.

FAQs

Common questions about damp.

General information to help you prepare for an enquiry. A survey is needed to determine what is appropriate for your property.

Is the damp survey free?

A2Z’s published service information offers surveys free of charge and without obligation. Contact A2Z directly to confirm availability.

Does every damp problem need a new DPC?

No. Condensation and inadequate airflow can produce similar symptoms. The appropriate solution depends on the findings of the survey.

What does DPC mean?

DPC stands for damp-proof course. It is intended to limit moisture movement through building fabric; the need for remedial DPC work depends on the property.

Can damaged plaster be repaired?

A2Z describes removing affected plaster, installing a membrane where appropriate and making good damage as part of an individual treatment plan.

How can I request a quotation?
