01 / Understanding the issue

Understanding the
causes of damp

Damp can appear similar on the surface while having different underlying causes. A professional survey helps identify the cause before treatment is selected.

What looks like damp may be condensation.

Moisture can collect on colder surfaces when ventilation and indoor conditions allow it. Understanding the environment is part of choosing an appropriate response.

↳ Educational examples only. The cause of moisture at a particular property requires a professional assessment.

03 / Damp & DPC

Damp treatment &
remedial works

The appropriate works depend on the survey findings. A2Z describes the following possible solutions in its published service information.

01 / VENTILATION

Improve airflow

Fit vents or make suitable ventilation improvements where condensation is linked to insufficient airflow.

02 / DPC

Damp-proof course

Consider internal DPC injection or external DPC works where the assessment indicates they are appropriate.

03 / WALL TREATMENT

Plaster & membranes

Remove affected plaster and install a suitable membrane where required by the agreed treatment plan.

04 / REPAIRS

Make good affected areas

Address the damage caused by damp as part of a solution tailored to the individual property.
